01 / About

A bridge between
technology and teams.

My career began in hands-on web development and grew into systems analysis and Scrum facilitation. That combination lets me understand both the technical details and the collaboration needed to deliver useful solutions.

“Good delivery happens when people have clarity, trust, and room to do their best work.”

Today, my work centers on helping teams plan, coordinate, and continuously improve. I facilitate Scrum ceremonies, support backlog refinement and sprint planning, help surface and resolve impediments, and work with product owners, business analysts, architects, and stakeholders to keep delivery moving.

My earlier development experience gives me a practical foundation for discussing requirements, feasibility, user-facing experiences, integrations, and implementation quality.
